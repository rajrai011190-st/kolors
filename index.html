Our Story
From a kitchen table
to your walls.

The Kolours started with Raj painting late into the night — acrylic on canvas, music on, no plan to sell. A few Instagram posts later, friends started asking to buy. That question — "is it for sale?" — became the seed of this marketplace.

We built The Kolours so that artists like Raj, Ankita, and Abhineet could list their original work without the overhead of a gallery or the noise of a massive platform. Every artist keeps most of what they earn. Every buyer gets a one-of-a-kind original, not a mass-produced print.

Based in Canada. Shipping Canada-wide.
